What happens next

  1. Complete the form and click Generate PDF.
  2. Check the preview, then download the PDF.

Before you register

  • Skilled applicants should provide relevant academic records or certificates to support suitable placement.
  • Non-skilled applicants must be physically fit for the work requested.
  • Manual registration at the GLCL office is also possible — call +233 303 961 288 to confirm a time.
  • Registration does not guarantee placement.
