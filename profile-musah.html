EXECUTIVE BIOGRAPHY & SUMMARY

Leading through decades of frontline maritime and stevedoring experience.

Mr. Alhassan Musah serves as the Managing Director, Operations Manager, and Board Director of Get Labour Company Limited. His career embodies a journey of maritime operational leadership—beginning on the port terminal floor as a tally and waybill clerk, rising to quay-side foreman and Chief Allocator at Safebond, serving as an Operations Supervisor at Silver Maritime Ghana Ltd, and ultimately founding GLCL in July 2013 to deliver dependable, structured workforce solutions across Ghana.

Shipping Management Quay-Side Stevedoring Labour Allocation Terminal Operations Occupational Safety (OSH) Port Logistics
1997 – 1998

Academic Foundations & Shipping Credentials

Completed early education at Wulensi L/A Primary and Wulensi JSS, followed by Business Secondary (Senior Secondary Certificate Examination, 1997). Graduated with a Diploma in Shipping Management from the Institute of Shipping and Management in 1998, laying the technical foundation for port operations and logistics administration.

2004 – 2006

Operational Foundations at Safebond

Joined Safebond Company Limited, working across core frontline terminal roles including tally clerk, waybill clerk, and delivery supervisor. Gained meticulous mastery over cargo tracking, container documentation, discharge records, and dispatch logistics.

2006 – 2010

Quay-Side Leadership & Chief Allocator

Promoted to quay-side foreman and ship-side labour officer at Safebond, supervising stevedores, crane transfers, and vessel discharge gangs directly at the berth. In 2010, appointed as Chief Allocator, orchestrating daily workforce matching, gang assignments, and roster coordination for complex marine terminal tasks.

2005 – 2010

Industry Workshops, Safety & Regulatory Training

Undertook intensive industry seminars and professional development programmes across Ghana:

  • Ghanaian Labour Laws and Industrial Dispute Management
  • Occupational Safety & Health (OSH) and Environmental Management
  • Hazardous Materials & Chemical Safety Protocols
  • Emergency Preparedness, Evacuation and Industrial Fire Safety
  • Transport Law, Axle Load & Vehicle Weight Regulations
  • Oil & Gas Marine Logistics Support
The lessons from these workshops became GLCL's standard: every worker on site wears the right protective gear.
2012 – 2014

Operations Supervision at Redsea & Silver Maritime

Joined Redsea Maritime Services on 1 April 2012. Progressed to serve as an Operations Officer and Operations Supervisor at Silver Maritime Ghana Ltd, directing marine logistics, terminal workflows, vessel clearance, and workforce deployment.

Operations Supervisor at Silver Maritime Ghana Ltd
July 2013 / June 2014 – Present

Founding & Directing Get Labour Company Limited

Established Get Labour Company Limited (GLCL) in July 2013 with the mission to elevate Ghana's labour supply through professionalism, safety, and dependability. On 5 June 2014, resigned from outside employment to take full-time executive leadership of GLCL as Operations Manager, Board Director, and Managing Director, building it into "Your Complete Labour Solution".
