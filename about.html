About Our Company

Connecting businesses requiring dependable manpower with individuals seeking employment opportunities across Ghana’s maritime, logistics, and industrial sectors.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

Dependable manpower, structured for the work that cannot wait.

Get Labour Company Limited (GLCL) is a labour services company established in July 2013 on Meridian Road, Tema, Ghana. We bridge the gap between businesses that need dependable manpower and capable people seeking structured work. GLCL supplies skilled and non-skilled labour for port, stevedoring and cargo-handling operations, supporting efficiency, productivity and long-term professional relationships.

Our Core Operating Principle: GLCL aims to respond to labour requests within 24–48 hours. This is an operational response aim, not a guarantee of placement, ensuring realistic and dependable turnaround.

OUR VISION

A Trusted Provider of Labour Solutions

To become a trusted provider of labour solutions, recognized across West Africa for dependable workforce support, uncompromising safety adherence, and professional service delivery.

OUR MISSION

Responsive & Needs-Based Workforce Supply

To connect businesses with suitable workers by providing responsive, reliable, and needs-based labour supply services while actively supporting sustainable access to productive employment opportunities for Ghanaian youth.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

The fundamental standards that govern every deployment, client relationship, and worker engagement at Get Labour Company Limited.

01. Reliability

Providing prompt, dependable workforce support that consistently responds to clients’ operational schedules, vessel berthing deadlines, and facility needs.

02. Professionalism

Maintaining disciplined, respectful, and transparent relationships with corporate clients, deployed personnel, port authorities, and business partners.

03. Integrity

Promoting ethical business dealings, prompt and transparent worker wage administration, and responsible labour practices without compromise.

04. Efficiency

Supporting clients in meeting urgent manpower requisitions in a timely, systematic, and well-organized manner, minimizing terminal dwell times.

05. Safety & Responsibility

Enforcing strict compliance with applicable workplace safety protocols, verified personal protective equipment (PPE), and hazard prevention on every job site.

HOW WE DELIVER

Structured Service Delivery Approach

We receive workforce requests from clients and match them with suitably qualified personnel through a simple 4-step process:

1. Request & Review: Receiving and thoroughly reviewing the client’s manpower requirements, job scope, and shift duration.
2. Identification: Matching technical proficiencies and allocating skilled certified equipment operators or general labour teams.
3. Coordinated Deployment: Managing worker assembly, safety verification, and timely transport to the port or facility.
4. On-site Communication: Maintaining ongoing coordination between clients, site supervisors, and deployed workforce.
