NATURE OF BUSINESS

Workforce Solutions Built for Real Demands

Certified equipment operators and disciplined stevedoring gangs for Ghana’s shipping lines, terminals and cargo facilities.

01 / QUALIFIED TECHNICAL & OPERATIONAL PERSONNEL

Skilled Labour Supply

Certified operators and supervisors for terminals and yards where precision and equipment safety matter. Every candidate is verified through licences, port experience and references, so they are ready to work from day one.

  • Reach Stacker Operators: Container stacking and vessel loading.
  • Forklift Operators: Pallet handling, container stuffing and warehouse storage.
  • Crane Operators: Quay gantry, mobile harbour and overhead cranes.
  • Supervisors & Foremen: Berth coordination, shifts and gang safety.
  • Tally & Documentation Clerks: Cargo manifests, waybills and gate audits.
  • Heavy Equipment Drivers: Terminal haulage and transit transport.
Certification Verification: All skilled operator requisitions are matched against candidates holding accredited machinery licenses and validated operating hours.

02 / DISCIPLINED GENERAL WORKFORCE

Non-Skilled & Stevedoring Gangs

Disciplined teams for cargo handling, vessel holds, warehouse sorting and heavy physical work, led by designated lead men for clear accountability and mobilised on time.

  • General Labourers: Warehouse stacking, sorting and packaging.
  • Ashore Labourers: Wharf handling, hook unlatching and apron sorting.
  • Transit Labourers: Corridor transfer, cross-docking and yard support.
  • Topside Labourers: Vessel hold rigging, hooks, hatch squaring and lashing.
  • Dedicated Shift Gangs: Teams matched to your shifts (day, night, weekend).
Worker Safety Protocol: Every gang member deployed through GLCL is required to wear standard personal protective equipment (steel-toe boots, helmets, high-vis vests).
